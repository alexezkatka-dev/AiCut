## K03 | step

TEXT: BUTTERFLY IT

## K04 | step
SHOT: overhead, hand sprinkling salt and pepper over two thin chicken pieces
VO: Salt both sides, and let it sit while the pan heats up.
RU: Посоли с обеих сторон и оставь, пока греется сковорода.

## K05 | step
SHOT: side angle, chicken pieces placed into a hot pan with oil, sizzling

TEXT: DON'T TOUCH IT
RU: Огонь выше среднего. Клади от себя и не трогай.

## K06 | step
SHOT: overhead, tongs flipping a golden chicken piece in the pan
